Authentic Ghanaian Jollof Rice

Ghanaian Jollof Rice is a rich, flavorful one-pot rice dish cooked in a tomato-based sauce and served with delicious chicken. It is a favorite at family gatherings, parties, and celebrations across Ghana.

Ingredients:

For The Jollof Rice:

  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 large tomatoes (blended)
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 large onion (chopped)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 Scotch bonnet pepper (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 seasoning cube

For The Chicken:

  • 4 chicken thighs or drumsticks
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

Equipments:

  • Large cooking pot
  • Frying pan (optional)
  • Blender
  • Wooden spoon
  • Chopping board
  • Sharp knife
  • Measuring cups
  • Serving plate

Instructions:

  1. Wash and season the chicken with the spices and salt.
  2. Fry or bake the chicken until golden brown.
  3. Blend the tomatoes, onion, garlic, ginger, and pepper.
  4. Heat oil in a large pot and fry the tomato paste for about 3 minutes.
  5. Add the blended mixture and cook until the sauce thickens.
  6. Add curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, seasoning cube, and salt.
  7. Pour in the chicken stock and bring to a boil.
  8. Add the washed rice and stir well.
  9. Cover the pot and cook on low heat until the rice is tender.
  10. Serve with the chicken and garnish with sliced vegetables if desired.

Cooking Tips:

  • 🍅 Use ripe tomatoes for a richer, sweeter sauce.
  • 🔥 Cook the rice over low heat to prevent burning.
  • 🍗 Marinate the chicken for at least 30 minutes before cooking for better flavor.
  • 🥄 Avoid stirring the rice too often, as this can make it mushy.
  • 🌿 Garnish with sliced onions, cucumber, or tomatoes for a fresh finish.

❓Frequently Asked Questions:

Can I use brown rice instead of white rice?

Yes. Brown rice works, but it takes longer to cook and may require additional stock or water.

How should I store leftover Jollof Rice?

Store it in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to three days. Reheat thoroughly before serving.

Can I make this recipe vegetarian?

Absolutely! Replace the chicken with vegetables or tofu and use vegetable stock instead of chicken stock.

Why is my Jollof Rice too soft?

This usually happens if too much liquid is added or the rice is overcooked. Measure your liquids carefully and cook over low heat.
