Refreshing Homemade Sobolo

Sobolo is an amazing antioxidizing snack. It's refreshing and packed with important vitamins and minerals. Often served cold and it can be paired with jollof rice

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups dried hibiscus petals
  • 2 liters water
  • 1 fresh ginger root (sliced)
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 4 cloves
  • Pineapple peels (optional)
  • ½ cup sugar or honey (adjust to taste)
  • Ice cubes
  • Lemon slices (optional)

Equipments:

  • Large cooking pot
  • Fine sieve or strainer
  • Wooden spoon
  • Measuring cup
  • Pitcher or jug
  • Knife
  • Chopping board
  • Refrigerator

Instructions:

  1. Rinse the hibiscus petals thoroughly.
  2. Pour the water into a large pot.
  3. Add the hibiscus petals, ginger, cinnamon, cloves, and pineapple peels.
  4. Bring to a boil.
  5. Reduce the heat and simmer for about 20 minutes.
  6. Remove from the heat and allow the drink to cool slightly.
  7. Strain the liquid into a clean pitcher.
  8. Stir in the sugar or honey until dissolved.
  9. Refrigerate until chilled.
  10. Serve over ice with a slice of lemon if desired.

Preparation Tips:

  • 🌺 Rinse the hibiscus petals well before boiling.
  • 🫚 Fresh ginger gives the drink a stronger, fresher flavor.
  • 🍍 Pineapple peels add a natural fruity sweetness.
  • ❄️ Chill the drink for several hours before serving for the best taste.
  • 🍯 Add sugar or honey gradually so you can adjust the sweetness to your liking.

❓Frequently Asked Questions:

Can I drink Sobolo without sugar?

Yes. You can enjoy it unsweetened or use natural sweeteners like honey if preferred.

How long does Sobolo last?

When refrigerated in a sealed container, it usually stays fresh for about three to five days.

Can I serve Sobolo warm?

Yes. Although it's commonly served cold, it can also be enjoyed warm, especially on cooler days.

Can I add other fruits?

Certainly! Many people add pineapple, oranges, cloves, cinnamon, or mint leaves to create different flavors.
